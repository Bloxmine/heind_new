# FPS game in the same graphical style as the rally game
- Realistic maps, based on screenshots provided. Realistic baked lighting, and shadow maps, but keeping that dithered cel shaded style.
- Gameplay like Unreal Tournament, fast paced movement. But with CoD sliding,
- Gameplay modes, 1v1, TDM, CTF.
- Weapons are wacky, but real objects.
- Multiplayer with p2p.
# Melee weapon ideas:
- Pool noodle, cheese slicer, rake, whip,
# Shoot weapon ideas:
- Baseball, water gun, and more funny weapons.
Powerups floating on the map like in UT, ammo, health pickups etc.
